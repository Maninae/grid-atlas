      How we know this

      Everything here comes from free, public US government data. No estimates of ours — just their numbers, drawn kindly.

      WhatSourceData year
      Fuel mix & 25-year trends US Energy Information Administration 2001–2025
      CO₂ per kWh, ZIP → grid region EPA eGRID 2023 (newest official)
      Hour-by-hour grid data EIA Hourly Grid Monitor 2025
      Power plant locations & sizes EIA US Energy Atlas 2026
      Electricity prices EIA retail sales 2001–2025

      Numbers count utility-scale generation (rooftop solar is tracked separately and not included). ZIP codes that straddle two grid regions get their main one, same as the EPA's own lookup. This site is a static page — your ZIP never leaves your browser.
